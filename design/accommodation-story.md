# Accommodation 建筑 — 故事与学习内容设计

> 目标用户：B1 级别留学生
> 设计理念：用一个连续的故事串联所有场景，让玩家在"追剧"中自然吸收语言

---

## 故事梗概

你是一个刚到英国的留学生，带着两个行李箱和一堆行李来到了一座陌生城市。你需要自己找房子、看房、签合同、搬进去、应对突发状况、处理室友关系——最终把一个空荡荡的房间变成"家"。

---

## 角色设定

| 角色 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **你（主角）** | 刚到的留学生，预算有限，英语 B1 水平 |
| **Mrs. Fletcher** | 房东老太太，说话快，有点难懂，人其实不坏 |
| **Sam** | 现有室友，本地人，研究生，看起来友善但生活习惯… |
| **Priya** | 另一个室友，印度留学生，比你早来半年，你的"老鸟" |
| **Jake** | 维修工，随叫随到，口头禅 "Should be sorted by Tuesday" |

## Unit 分章设计（7 章）

### Unit 1 — "Two Suitcases and Nowhere to Sleep"
**学习主题**：Reading rental listings, initial inquiries
**故事**：你拖着行李出了火车站，手机地图上一个箭头指着一个叫 "Oakfield Road" 的街道。Airbnb 太贵了，你得自己找长期租房。你开始刷各种网站，打电话咨询，发现每个 listing 的缩写和术语都像密码。

**重点词汇**：
- 房屋类型：flat, apartment, studio, bedsit, terraced house, semi-detached
- 描述词：spacious, cosy, furnished, unfurnished, en-suite, shared
- 缩写解码：ETB（electricity/toilet/bathroom? 不是，是 each term bill）, PCM（per calendar month）, DSS, EPC rating
- 实用句型：
  - "Is this still available?"
  - "What does 'all bills included' cover?"
  - "When would the room be ready?"

---

### Unit 2 — "The View That Wasn't What It Seemed"
**学习主题**：Viewing a flat, asking the right questions, describing spaces
**故事**：你约了三个看房。第一个照片精美，实地一看窗户外面是堵墙；第二个暖气坏了房东说 "just put on a jumper"。第三次你看到了 Oakfield Road 32B —— 房间不大但阳光不错。

**重点词汇**：
- 看房用语：viewing, deposit, advance rent, references, guarantor, tenancy
- 空间描述：ground floor, top floor, facing, overlooking, natural light, drafty, damp
- 提问句型：
  - "How long has it been on the market?"
  - "Is the heating gas or electric?"
  - "Would the landlord be open to…?"
  - "What's the neighbourhood like?"

---

### Unit 3 — "Mrs. Fletcher's Fine Print"
**学习主题**：Understanding contracts, legal terminology, negotiation
**故事**：Mrs. Fletcher 拿着一份合同出现了，她说 "Just the standard boilerplate, dear." 你看着上面密密麻麻的条款，手心开始出汗。你想起 Priya 说的："永远别直接签，至少问三个问题。"

**重点词汇**：
- 合同术语：tenancy agreement, fixed-term, periodic, break clause, deposit protection, eviction notice
- 权利义务：landlord's responsibility, tenant's obligation, joint liability, subletting
- 关键表达：
  - "Could you clarify this clause?"
  - "What happens if I need to leave early?"
  - "Is the deposit protected under a scheme?"
  - "I'd like to add an addendum about…"

---

### Unit 4 — "Home Sweet Empty Room"
**学习主题**：Furniture, utilities, moving in, setting up
**故事**：签完合同，你拿到了钥匙。你打开门——房间是空的。没有床，没有桌子，连个灯都没有。Priya 发来消息："Welcome to British renting, mate. IKEA is your friend." 现在你需要搞定一切。

**重点词汇**：
- 家具：mattress, wardrobe, desk, shelving, bedside table, curtains
- 水电燃气：utility bills, council tax, standing charge, meter reading, broadband, direct debit
- 实用句型：
  - "I need to set up the utilities in my name."
  - "Can I get the meter reading from the previous tenant?"
  - "Who do I contact about the broadband connection?"
  - "When does the council tax bill arrive?"

---

### Unit 5 — "Sam's Midnight Kitchen Experiments"
**学习主题**：Flatmate dynamics, setting boundaries, household routines
**故事**：入住第一周你发现 Sam 有几个特点：第一，他凌晨两点还在厨房做咖喱；第二，他的盘子永远在水槽里而不是洗碗机里；第三，他觉得 "打扫公共区域" 是个可选项。你需要跟他谈谈。

**重点词汇**：
- 家庭事务：chores, rota, washing up, bin day, recycling, communal area, quiet hours
- 习惯描述：messy, considerate, inconsiderate, passive-aggressive, laid-back
- 沟通句型：
  - "Do you mind if we set up a cleaning rota?"
  - "I'm not trying to be difficult, but…"
  - "Would it be possible to…?"
  - "I get it's not a big deal to you, but for me it is."

---

### Unit 6 — "The Leak"
**学习主题**：Reporting maintenance issues, dealing with emergencies, following up
**故事**：一个雨夜，天花板上开始渗水。你给 Mrs. Fletcher 发了消息，她说 "Jake will come round." Jake 确实来了，他说 "Should be sorted by Tuesday." 现在是周四，水还在滴。

**重点词汇**：
- 维修相关：leak, burst pipe, boiler breakdown, damp, mould, fuse box, radiator
- 紧急程度：urgent, emergency, non-urgent, health hazard
- 沟通句型：
  - "There's a leak coming from the ceiling above my room."
  - "I reported this on Monday, any update?"
  - "Is this covered by the landlord's insurance?"
  - "I need this resolved ASAP — there's mould forming."

---

### Unit 7 — "House Meeting"
**学习主题**：Conflict resolution, compromise, building community
**故事**：Priya 提议开个 "house meeting"。Sam 说他愿意洗碗如果你不介意他周末带朋友来。Mrs. Fletcher 同意换个新床垫如果你签第二年的合同。你们一起制定了 "house rules"，还一起做了顿晚饭。这个奇怪的小组合，慢慢像一个家了。

**重点词汇**：
- 关系与情感：compromise, respect, mutual, trust, tension, breakthrough, settle in
- 家庭规则：guests policy, noise curfew, shared groceries, visitors overnight
- 高级表达：
  - "I think we can meet halfway on this."
  - "From my perspective, the issue is…"
  - "I appreciate you bringing this up."
  - "Let's put it in writing so we're all clear."

## 每个 Unit 的课程节点设计（示例：Unit 1）

每个 Unit 拆成 3 个课：

| 节点 | 形式 | 内容 |
|------|------|------|
| 📖 Story Scene | 沉浸式叙事 | 故事推进 + 关键对话，玩家阅读并选择对话选项 |
| 🗣️ Vocabulary Drill | 词汇练习 | 本单元 8-12 个核心词汇的配对/填空/听力 |
| 💬 Role Play | 情景对话 | 模拟真实场景（打电话问房源/回复房东消息） |
